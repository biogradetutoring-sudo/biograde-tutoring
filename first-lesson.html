Always 1:1 · always discounted

Your first lesson

Every student starts the same way, no matter which plan you eventually join — one discounted 1:1 session, so lessons afterwards are built around exactly where you're starting from.

FORMAT & PRICE

1:1, and £10 off

The first lesson is always one-to-one, priced at £8 instead of the usual £18 — even if you're planning to join a group plan afterwards. This keeps the risk low while we figure out if it's a good fit.

WHAT HAPPENS IN IT

A proper starting point, not a sales pitch

We'll talk through where things currently stand — topics that feel shaky, what's coming up in class, and your goal grade — then work through a short practice question together so you can see exactly how sessions are run and what the feedback style looks like.

AFTER THE SESSION

No obligation either way

Once it's done, you decide whether to continue — 1:1, a weekday small group, or the weekend group — or not at all. Nothing is automatically booked beyond the first session.

PAYMENT

PayPal, for now

Payment is currently taken via PayPal, sent after the session is confirmed. More payment options may be added later.

TIMING

Fits around a weekly timetable

Ongoing plans run on a fixed weekly timetable (see the Plans page), and weekday slots are usually scheduled between 4–5pm. Your first lesson time is arranged directly once you get in touch.
